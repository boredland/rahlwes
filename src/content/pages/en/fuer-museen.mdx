## Three steps to the exhibition

<Illustration src="/uploads/museen-forschen.webp" alt={"Two women, one with glasses holding a box of books, the other looking through a microscope, in between a photo of a teapot, all in a collage style with graphic elements."} />

### Research

I research historical topics, families and biographies, places and buildings as well as the history of objects. These include paintings, sculptures, porcelain and silver. The focus is on the history of Western Europe in the 19th and 20th centuries.

I conduct research for you in archives, libraries and museums throughout Europe. On request, I can search internationally for key documents and images, sound and film recordings and exhibition objects for your project. You benefit from my knowledge of English and French. I also carry out fact checks for texts, documentaries and films on historical topics. My results are scientifically sound and verifiable.

<Illustration src="/uploads/museen-verstehen.webp" alt={"Graphic with a blue teapot, a hand holding an orange notebook and a hand holding a wooden spoon."} />

### Understand

Written documents, works of art and everyday objects can only be understood in their historical context: Which manufactory does the tea service come from? What does the decoration show? Is the previous owner known? On what occasions was it uncovered? What do we learn about the previous owner's self-image and lifestyle?

I help you to trace the history of memorabilia and collection objects and decipher their meaning. I decipher old manuscripts and date photographs. I place key documents such as letters and diaries in their historical context. I present my findings in a comprehensible and understandable way.

<Illustration src="/uploads/museen-vermitteln.webp" alt={"A museum or art gallery exhibition space with a person holding a smartphone. The person is looking at a painting while the cell phone shows a digital version."} />

### Mediate

How we tell history determines how it is remembered. That's why good knowledge communication is important to me. Based on my research findings, I develop innovative and target group-oriented content and concepts for exhibitions. I communicate sensitive topics and complex historical contexts simply and clearly.
