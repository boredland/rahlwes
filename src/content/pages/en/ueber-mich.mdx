**Hello, my name is Ann-Kathrin Rahlwes.**

I have been working as a freelance historian since 2013. I help families, companies and museums to research and reappraise historical topics. I combine object histories with people and their lives to find out who they were, how they lived and what was important to them.

I want to get people excited about history. That is why it is important to me to convey history in a clear and understandable way for all age groups. This also has to do with my involvement in historical and political education from 2003 to 2013. I completed my master's degree in political science, medieval and modern history and philosophy in 2011.

## Lectures & Events

### Gaming in the museum. Communicating analog and digital in a playful way (17.02.25)

*Museumsverband Hessen*

In the workshop, participants from Hessian museums learned how playful learning works in museums and which formats are suitable for their needs. Numerous examples from European museums provided suggestions on how different educational objectives can be implemented. A digital follow-up meeting facilitated a collegial exchange and provided inspiration for further work.

[More about the event](https://museumsverband-hessen.de/veranstaltung/gaming-im-museum/)

### Communicating object history (11.10.24)

*Museumsverband Hessen*

The aim of the full-day workshop was to train museum employees to identify objects in their museum collection that are well suited to conveying knowledge about historical events, biographies and topics as part of digital education programs. Using the example of posts for Instagram and digital exhibitions for Google Arts & Culture, participants learned how storytelling methods can be implemented.

[More about the event](https://museumsverband-hessen.de/veranstaltung/objektgeschichte-vermitteln/)

### Biographical learning with the short film *The FAMILY WAS JEWISH ...*

*Fritz Bauer Institute*

The teacher training introduced the new teaching material for the short film "DIE FAMILIE WAR JÜDISCH ...", which I developed on behalf of the Fritz Bauer Institute.
