I develop material for school lessons as well as for the preparation and follow-up of museum visits.

## What is created

- Worksheets and material booklets for different year groups
- Accompanying material for films and exhibitions
- Further training for teachers

## References

On behalf of the Fritz Bauer Institute, I developed the teaching material for the short film "DIE FAMILIE WAR JÜDISCH ...". Together with Ricarda Wawra, I created the material booklet "Freiheitsrechte. The example of Anne Frank's ancestors and family".
