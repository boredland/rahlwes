I support museums in making historical sources scientifically accessible, selecting exhibits and developing inclusive exhibition concepts. I convey complex historical topics and object histories in a way that is appropriate for the target group.

## Giessen as a place of refuge for international asylum seekers.

The learning and memorial site Notaufnahmelager Gießen sheds light on the history of one of Germany's central reception centers from 1946 to 2018.

I designed several themed walls for the permanent exhibition, ranging from historical experiences of flight to current debates about Europe's external borders. I researched the historical context, searched for image and media sources, evaluated statistics and expert interviews and helped the museum to vividly convey complex global contexts, such as the consequences of climate change on refugees.

## We are now. Jewish Frankfurt from the Enlightenment to the Present.

The Jewish Museum Frankfurt am Main opened its new permanent exhibition in October 2020, displaying selected items from the Frank Family Center's collection. Everyday objects, paintings, photos, books and letters tell of the lives of Anne Frank's ancestors and family. I researched the objects and biographies and supported the museum in conveying the stories in a way that was appropriate for the target group.

## "In demand: Frankfurt and the NS."

The exhibition of the Young Museum of the Historical Museum Frankfurt am Main (December 9, 2021 - February 4, 2024) is aimed at people aged 10 and over to introduce them to the topic of National Socialism in an age-appropriate way. I researched the biographies of children and young people who lived in Frankfurt am Main during the National Socialist era.

## Inherited. Bought. Robbed?

Everyday things and their Nazi past

From May 17 to October 14, 2018, the Historisches Museum Frankfurt am Main exhibited the results of its provenance research on the painting and silver collection. I researched the biographies of dispossessed Jewish families and supported the museum in selecting exhibits and developing an exhibition concept.
