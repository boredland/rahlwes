Based on my research findings, I develop content and concepts for digital formats - tailored to the target group and suitable for the format.

## Formats

- Digital exhibitions, for example with Google Arts and Culture
- Contributions for social media, especially Instagram
- Content for apps such as the Frankfurt History App
- Digital games and playful mediation formats

## References

For the Historisches Museum Frankfurt, I developed content for the Frankfurt History App, which makes historical places in the city accessible.
