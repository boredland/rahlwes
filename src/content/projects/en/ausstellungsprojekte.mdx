I research the history of objects, develop innovative and target group-oriented exhibition concepts, find unique exhibits and clarify usage and image rights.

## What I contribute

- Research on objects, biographies and events in archives and collections
- Inclusive and participative exhibition concepts
- Selection and procurement of exhibits
- Clarification of usage and image rights
- Exhibition texts that are comprehensible and precise

## References

Most recently, I helped design the permanent exhibition at the Gießen emergency reception center as a place of learning and remembrance and was responsible for communicating the recent history of migration.
